Smart Campus is a trading platform built for Ontario high school students to buy, sell, rent, and exchange school supplies locally, making school essentials more affordable and sustainable for everyone.

**Key Features**
- Student Verified Access: Students are required to sign in with their email and select their school to gain access to a secure marketplace specific for their school community
- Flexible Seller Options: Sellers can choose between selling, renting, and exchanging their items, ensuring convenience and accounting for all circumstances
- Targeted Search: Filters and tags (e.g., course codes, price ranges, etc.) ensure that buyers find what they want and sellers reach their target audience
- Buyer-Seller Chat: Real-time chat feature enables easy communication and coordination of item pickup between buyers and sellers
- Mutually Agreed Transactions: Transactions are completely voluntary and may only go through after both the seller and the buyer confirm their decisions on the platform
- Secure Transaction System: Offers a variety of safe and reliable payment methods, including credit / debit and cash on pickup

**Usage Guide**
